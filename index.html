Section 122 Explained

Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 authorizes the President to impose a temporary import surcharge of up to 15% for a maximum of 150 days to address large and serious U.S. balance-of-payments deficits.

Following the Supreme Court's February 2026 ruling in Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump, the Administration invoked Section 122 to impose a 10% global tariff effective February 24, 2026 through July 24, 2026.

Goods already subject to Section 232 tariffs are excluded and are not subject to both duties simultaneously.
